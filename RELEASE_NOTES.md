The paper studies sharp actual Klein four subgroup counts on the maximum-involution branch of the specified semi-extraspecial class. The complete lower-equality isomorphism classification covers **even half-dimension n >= 4** only. It does not claim unique core embeddings. The sign restriction concerns the core and the entire remainder; two negative initial planes can together form a positive remainder.
